Multi-Tenant Architecture

Engineered for Multi-School Scale & Complete Data Isolation

Grexotix School Management Platform is architected from the ground up to support multiple independent educational institutions on a secure, unified SaaS foundation.

🏛️

Independent School Workspaces

Each onboarded school operates in its own isolated environment with dedicated academic years, custom classes, custom fee schedules, and independent institutional branding.

🔒

Server-Side Tenant Isolation

Zero data leakage. Every API request and query is bound to the authenticated school identity. One school's administrators, teachers, or parents can never view another school's records.

👥

Strict Role Governance

Clear operational boundaries between Grexotix Platform Admins (school review & activation), School Super Admins (institutional control), Teaching Faculty, and Parents.

📱

Cross-Platform Ecosystem

Consistent, responsive experience across responsive Desktop WebApp, native Android APK, and iOS with synchronized real-time RFID and GPS tracking.
